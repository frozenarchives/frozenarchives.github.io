<p>
👋 Hey there! I'm a PhD candidate studying the dynamics of the Antarctic Ice Sheets
over the past 1.4 million years using Ice-Rafted Debris (IRD) from deep-sea sediment
cores. I use a combination of machine learning, radiogenic isotopic analysis, and
iceberg trajectory modeling to understand past Antarctic ice-sheet behavior.
</p>

<p>I'm particularly interested in:</p>

<ul>
  <li>IRD flux variability across the Mid-Pleistocene Transition (MPT)</li>
  <li>Provenance studies using Sr, Nd, and Pb isotopes</li>
  <li>Reconstructing iceberg trajectories to better understand past ocean circulation patterns</li>
</ul>

<p>
My samples come from
<a href="http://publications.iodp.org/proceedings/383/383title.html">
IODP Expedition 383 – Dynamics of the Pacific Antarctic Circumpolar Current (DYNAPACC)</a>,
an international research cruise that targeted sediment cores across the Central South
Pacific sector of the Southern Ocean. The expedition recovered high-resolution sediment
cores that are ideal for reconstructing past atmosphere-ocean dynamics of the ACC and
Antarctic ice-sheet dynamics across key climate transitions such as the MPT.
</p>

<p>
You can usually find me coding in Python (mostly in JupyterLab), working with deep-sea
sediment cores, or building visualizations and numerical models of the Southern Ocean
and Antarctica.
</p>
