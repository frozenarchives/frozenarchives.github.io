About Me

👋 Hey there! I'm a PhD candidate studying the dynamics of the Antarctic Ice Sheets over the past 1.4 million years using Ice-Rafted Debris (IRD) from deep-sea sediment cores. I combine machine learning, radiogenic isotopic analysis, and iceberg trajectory modeling to investigate how Antarctic ice sheets have responded to past climate change.

I'm particularly interested in:

  • IRD flux variability across the Mid-Pleistocene Transition (MPT)
  • Provenance studies using Sr, Nd, and Pb isotopes
  • Reconstructing iceberg trajectories and their relationship with past ocean circulation

My samples come from IODP Expedition 383 – Dynamics of the Pacific Antarctic Circumpolar Current (DYNAPACC), which recovered sediment cores from the Central South Pacific sector of the Southern Ocean. These archives provide an opportunity to investigate Antarctic ice-sheet dynamics and Southern Ocean circulation across major climate transitions such as the MPT.

You can usually find me coding in Python (mostly in JupyterLab), working with deep-sea sediment cores, or building visualizations and numerical models of Antarctica and the Southern Ocean.

📍 Currently based at University of Delaware.
