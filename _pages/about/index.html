👋 Hey there! I'm a PhD candidate studying the dynamics of the Antarctic Ice Sheets over the past 1.4 million years using Ice-Rafted Debris (IRD) from deep-sea sediment cores. I use a combination of machine learning, radiogenic isotopic analysis, and iceberg trajectory modeling to understand past Antarctic ice-sheet behavior.

I'm particularly interested in:

  • IRD flux variability across the Mid-Pleistocene Transition (MPT)
  • Provenance studies using Sr, Nd, and Pb isotopes
  • Reconstructing iceberg trajectories to better understand past ocean circulation patterns

My samples come from IODP Expedition 383 – Dynamics of the Pacific Antarctic Circumpolar Current (DYNAPACC), an international research cruise that targeted sediment cores across the Central South Pacific sector of the Southern Ocean. The expedition recovered high-resolution sediment cores that are ideal for reconstructing past atmosphere-ocean dynamics of the ACC and Antarctic ice-sheet dynamics across key climate transitions such as the MPT.

You can usually find me coding in Python (mostly in JupyterLab), working with deep-sea sediment cores, or building visualizations and numerical models of the Southern Ocean and Antarctica.

📍 Currently based at University of Delaware.
