Projects

  • 2023 - Present
    Antarctic Ice Sheet Dynamics Across the Mid-Pleistocene Transition
    Investigation of Antarctic Ice Sheet dynamics using high-resolution Ice-Rafted Debris records from the Southern Ocean.
    • Deep learning-based instance segmentation of IRD from X-ray scans
    • High-resolution IRD flux reconstruction spanning approximately 1.4 million years
    • Time-series analysis of Antarctic IRD variability
    • Investigation of IRD delivery to ice-distal locations
  • 2026 - Present
    Ice Sheet and Iceberg Trajectory Modeling
    Development of an integrated modeling framework linking Antarctic ice sheet calving, iceberg size distributions, iceberg trajectories, breakup, and meltwater distribution.
    • Community Ice Sheet Model (CISM) simulations of Antarctic calving rates
    • Generation of iceberg size distributions from modeled calving rates
    • Iceberg trajectory modeling for modern and past climates
    • Modeling breakup of giant icebergs and meltwater distribution
    • Validation using radiogenic isotope analyses of Antarctic IRD grains
  • 2023 - Present
    AI/ML-Based IRD Detection
    Development of machine learning and deep learning approaches for automated detection and quantification of Ice-Rafted Debris in sediment core X-ray scans.
    • Deep learning-based instance segmentation
    • Automated IRD identification and counting
    • High-resolution IRD time-series generation
  • 2024 - Present
    Antarctic IRD Provenance Using Radiogenic Isotopes
    Determination of potential Antarctic source regions of iceberg-rafted material using radiogenic isotope compositions of IRD grains.
    • Strontium (Sr), Neodymium (Nd), and Lead (Pb) isotope analysis
    • Sample preparation and chemical separation
    • Radiogenic isotope analysis of Antarctic IRD grains
